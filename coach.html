Privacy

TFT Coach reads your game on this computer only: it has no account and no server of its own. Overwolf, the platform it runs on, collects anonymous usage statistics.

About

Welcome to TFT Coach

A companion for Teamfight Tactics that helps you read your economy and the meta.

  • Meta comps: the comps doing best in recent high-rank games, with their units and items.
  • Live game: start a match and this view fills in with your gold, level and interest numbers, the meta comp closest to your board, and the comps your opponents play.
  • Home: a summary of your last game.

Put this window on a second monitor, or switch to it between rounds.

TFT Coach runs on Overwolf. Overwolf and its ad vendors may store and read information on this device, such as a device identifier, to measure usage and to choose ads. TFT Coach shows no ads today. You can choose what you allow now, or later from Settings.
